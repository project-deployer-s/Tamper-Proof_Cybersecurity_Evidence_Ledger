Check that a message is genuine and unchanged

Every message is signed by its sender. The receiver can check the signature at any time, and every check is written to a ledger that nobody can quietly edit.

  1. Sign. The sender's private key signs the message in their browser.
  2. Send. The server stores the message and records it in the ledger.
  3. Check. The receiver checks sender, content and signature, and gets a clear verdict.
